# Translation review list

Questionable decisions and cross-mod conflicts in the Ukrainian pack, collected for later review.
Snapshot of 2026-10-11 (pack 1.3.0, mods in `mods/` at that time). Nothing here is applied automatically.
Translations of mods that are no longer installed stay in the pack; the few that would override vanilla text
(or a newer translation) are parked in [`inactive-translations.cfg`](inactive-translations.cfg) (not shipped).
